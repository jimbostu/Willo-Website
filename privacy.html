Privacy Policy
for Willo Idea Lab apps

Last updated: November 24, 2026

01Overview

This privacy policy applies to the mobile apps developed by Willo Idea Lab LLC (each, "the App"):

  • SnapSauce
  • GetaFive: AP World
  • Ivy Score

It explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use the App. Where an app works differently, that is noted below.

02Information We Collect

Our apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. They have no accounts or sign-in, and they do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, or precise location. App data is stored on your device and is never sent to our servers.

Ivy Score is the one app that uses photos: it uses your camera or a photo you choose to read a game board. That photo is analyzed only on your device and is never sent to us; see “Camera, Photos, and iCloud (Ivy Score)” below.

No cookies or tracking. This website and the pages our apps open do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. Willo Idea Lab does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and does not share data with data brokers.

03Data Storage

App data is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data is not accessible to us. For example:

  • SnapSauce: preferences, favorites, and statistics.
  • GetaFive: AP World: study progress (XP, streaks, mastery, and badges) and settings. You can erase it at any time with “Erase progress” in Settings.
  • Ivy Score: your saved games (scores, goals and challenges earned, and a photo of each finished board). These can also sync through your own iCloud account, as described below.

Deleting an app removes its data from your device. For Ivy Score, games synced to your iCloud account stay there and return if you reinstall the App, until you delete them.

04Camera, Photos, and iCloud (Ivy Score)

  • Camera and photos: Ivy Score asks for camera access only so you can photograph a finished game, or you can choose an existing photo instead. The photo is analyzed entirely on your device to recognize the game; it is never uploaded to us or to any server we operate. The App can only see the photos you pick.
  • iCloud sync: Ivy Score uses Apple’s iCloud to sync your saved games, including their photos, to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This data is stored in your private iCloud storage, which we cannot access. There is no separate sign-in.
  • Your control: you can delete individual games or all progress in the App (Score History). You can turn off iCloud syncing for Ivy Score in your device’s Settings under your name › iCloud; the App then keeps your games on the device only.

05Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID or an app-specific identifier)
  • IP address
  • General location data (based on IP)
  • App usage data and interaction with ads
  • Device information (model, operating system)
  • Diagnostic and performance data

This data is collected by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. For more information on how Google uses data, please visit policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

GetaFive: AP World

GetaFive does not track you. It requests non-personalized ads only and never asks for permission to track, so Apple does not give it or Google your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA). It also limits ads to content suitable for teens. Ads appear only between games, never during one. If you buy “Remove Ads,” the App no longer loads Google’s advertising software at all.

Ivy Score

Ivy Score does not track you. It shows a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen, requests non-personalized ads only, and never asks for permission to track, so Apple does not give it or Google your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA). Ads are not based on your identity or activity, and your photos and saved games are never shared with Google.

06In-App Purchases

GetaFive: AP World offers an optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details; Apple tells the App only whether the purchase was made, so that it can be honored and restored on your other devices.

07Your Choices and Rights

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland: where required by law, the App asks for your consent through Google’s consent tool before ads are shown. You can decline. In GetaFive, you can change your choice later in Settings under “Ad privacy choices.” In Ivy Score, use “Ad Privacy Choices” at the bottom of the home screen.
  • U.S. state privacy laws (such as California’s): we do not sell your personal information. GetaFive and Ivy Score do not share personal information for targeted advertising, because they request non-personalized ads only.
  • Everyone: you can delete all app data by deleting the App. In GetaFive, you can also avoid ads entirely by buying “Remove Ads.” In Ivy Score, use “Delete All Progress” in Score History to remove your games from your device and your iCloud account.

Because we do not hold any personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our side. Ivy Score data in your iCloud account is controlled by you through the App and your Apple Account. For data collected by Google, see Google’s privacy policy linked above.

08Children's Privacy

Our apps do not collect personal information from children under 13. The third-party advertising service (Google AdMob) may collect data as described above. GetaFive: AP World is designed for high school students preparing for the AP® World History: Modern exam and is not directed to children under 13. Ivy Score is a scoring tool for a board game, intended for a general audience, and is not directed to children under 13.
