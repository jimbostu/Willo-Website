Privacy Policy
for Willo Idea Lab apps

Last updated: October 3, 2026

01Overview

This privacy policy applies to the mobile apps developed by Willo Idea Lab LLC (each, "the App"):

  • SnapSauce
  • GetaFive: AP World

It explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use the App. Where an app works differently, that is noted below.

02Information We Collect

Our apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. They have no accounts or sign-in, and they do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. All user data is stored locally on your device and is never sent to any server.

03Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data is not accessible to us and is not transmitted anywhere. For example:

  • SnapSauce: preferences, favorites, and statistics.
  • GetaFive: AP World: study progress (XP, streaks, mastery, and badges) and settings. You can erase it at any time with “Erase progress” in Settings.

Deleting an app removes its data from your device.

04Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID or an app-specific identifier)
  • IP address
  • General location data (based on IP)
  • App usage data and interaction with ads
  • Device information (model, operating system)
  • Diagnostic and performance data

This data is collected by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. For more information on how Google uses data, please visit policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

GetaFive: AP World

GetaFive requests non-personalized ads only, does not ask for permission to track you across other apps, and limits ads to content suitable for teens. Ads appear only between games, never during one. If you buy “Remove Ads,” the App no longer loads Google’s advertising software at all.

05In-App Purchases

GetaFive: AP World offers an optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details; Apple tells the App only whether the purchase was made, so that it can be honored and restored on your other devices.

06Your Choices and Rights

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland: where required by law, the App asks for your consent through Google’s consent tool before ads are shown. You can decline. In GetaFive, you can change your choice later in Settings under “Ad privacy choices.”
  • U.S. state privacy laws (such as California’s): we do not sell your personal information. GetaFive does not share personal information for targeted advertising, because it requests non-personalized ads only.
  • Everyone: you can delete all app data by deleting the App. In GetaFive, you can also avoid ads entirely by buying “Remove Ads.”

Because we do not hold any personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our side. For data collected by Google, see Google’s privacy policy linked above.

07Children's Privacy

Our apps do not collect personal information from children under 13. The third-party advertising service (Google AdMob) may collect data as described above. GetaFive: AP World is designed for high school students preparing for the AP® World History: Modern exam and is not directed to children under 13.

08Trademarks

AP® is a registered trademark of the College Board, which is not affiliated with, and does not endorse, GetaFive: AP World.

09Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, including when we release new apps. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

10Contact
